# 4.3 RQ2: Regional Heterogeneity and Shortcut Boundaries

Week10 freezes RQ2 as a boundary-aware finding rather than a claim of pure structural transfer. The corrected E5 feature-bucket diagnostics show that the France transfer setting is strong under full features, but that strength is materially assisted by Website lexical and ccTLD/local-TLD signals. In particular, removing ccTLD/local-TLD information lowers T3 performance, and the lexical-only condition remains much stronger than the graph-only condition.

The paper-safe claim is therefore: regional heterogeneity is real, and high full-feature transfer performance may combine structural commonality with jurisdiction-specific lexical boundaries. T3 France should be reported as shortcut-sensitive transfer, not as evidence that the graph encoder alone captures a region-invariant illegal-site structure.

Forbidden interpretation: do not write that T3 France proves structural generalization, and do not convert lexical/ccTLD assistance into a model success claim.
